# Code of conduct

Serve follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, keep criticism about the work and not the
person, and leave no room for harassment of any kind. This applies in issues, discussions,
pull requests and every other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers through
[a private report](https://github.com/serve-bd/serve/security/advisories/new) or by contacting
[@shahriyardx](https://github.com/shahriyardx) directly. Reports are handled in confidence.
Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads or block people who do not follow this code.
